Companies:
    - Product Based
        - examples: Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Facebook, Netflix, Tesla, Zoho, Adobe, Salesforce, etc.
        - product based companies are those which create and sell their own products or services directly to consumers or other businesses.
        - salary range: 4 LPA to 50 LPA or more depending on the role, experience, and company.
        - hiring process: 
            - advanced quantitative and analytical skills (mcqs) or computer science fundamentals (mcqs)
            - coding rounds (data structures and algorithms) - 2 to 4 rounds
            - system design round (for experienced hires)
            - advanced programming rounds (for experienced hires)
            - technical interviews
            - behavioral interviews or HR interviews

    - Service Based
        - examples: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL, Accenture, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, LTI, Mindtree, etc.
        - service based companies provide services such as consulting, IT services, outsourcing, and business process management to other companies.
        - salary range: 1.8 LPA to 15 LPA depending on the role, experience, and company.
        - hiring process:
            - basic quantitative and analytical skills (mcqs) or computer science fundamentals (mcqs)
            - mostly communication based rounds (group discussions, personal interviews)
            - some companies might have coding rounds (data structures and algorithms) - 1 to 2 rounds
            - Resume based technical or personal interviews
            - behavioral interviews or HR interviews
